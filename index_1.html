The philosophy

AI does the work.
People stay in charge.

aidev is the way Microgame builds software in the age of AI. AI agents plan, build and test. Our people describe what is needed, check the result and decide what ships. Faster work, same care, and always a human hand on the wheel.

What we believe

Four ideas behind aidev

Humans decide

AI proposes, people approve. Responsibility never moves to a machine.

Think first, then build

Every piece of work starts from a clear plan, so the result matches what was asked.

Quality is not optional

Tests, traceability and clear errors are built in from day one, not added later.

Every lesson counts

What we learn on one project becomes a rule for the next. The whole team gets better together.

We did not change what makes Microgame.
We changed how we build it.
